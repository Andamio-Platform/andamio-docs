The Andamio app is the hosted product where learning and collaboration happen on-chain. You create an Andamio identity, take courses and earn credentials, and run credential-gated projects with contributors, all backed by the Cardano blockchain.

**Use the app:** [mainnet.app.andamio.io](https://mainnet.app.andamio.io)

The app teaches you how to operate it as you go. Course owners, project owners, and contributors are guided through their workflows in the product itself, so this page stays a light orientation rather than a full manual.

## Get oriented

- [Getting Started](/docs/apps-tooling/andamio-app/explore-getting-started) — create your on-chain identity.
- [Roles Overview](/docs/apps-tooling/andamio-app/roles) — the platform roles and what each can do.

## Build your own

The app is a forkable template. To stand up your own Andamio-powered app, start from [andamio-app-template](https://github.com/Andamio-Platform/andamio-app-template), a Next.js 15 / tRPC / Mesh SDK template. Login and sponsored transactions are handled for you.
