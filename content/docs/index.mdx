# Andamio Protocol Documentation

Andamio is a protocol that enables distributed work across organizations of any size. Anyone can mint credentials on the Andamio Protocol. Contributors use these credentials to unlock new opportunities. Credentials can be shared across project teams, enabling the fluid movement of talented people among impactful teams.

## Example:

1. An organization needs a social media manager for a product launch.
2. They create a credential for the social media role.
3. A contributor completes an assignment and earns the credential. They learn about the project and choose their path.
4. The contributor joins the project, completes tasks, and earns rewards based on their role.
5. The organization verifiably distributes rewards and generates impact reports.
6. Contributors leverage their credentials for new opportunities. Developers showcase code; social media managers highlight campaign success. Cross-disciplinary relationships form naturally.

### All of this is possible with Andamio, and here is what's most important: 

Anyone can plug the tools of Andamio into any application, building new organizational tools, new social networks, and new ways to collaborate.

Andamio is a protocol for decentralized treasury management and contribution tracking that enables transparent project coordination on the Cardano blockchain. It provides tools for contributor onboarding, on-chain credentialing, permissionless reputation, and accountable fund allocation that empower teams to collaborate with high levels of transparency and trust.
