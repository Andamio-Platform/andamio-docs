# Andamio Protocol

Andamio unlocks fluid talent movement across organizations. Mint credentials, verify skills, and connect contributors to opportunities—all on-chain.

## How It Works

1. Organization creates a credential for a needed role
2. Contributor completes an assignment and earns the credential
3. Contributor joins projects and completes tasks
4. Organization distributes rewards with transparent verification
5. Contributors build portfolios across disciplines and projects

### The Power of Andamio

Build anything. Integrate everywhere. Andamio's tools plug into any application—creating new organizational systems, social networks, and collaboration methods.

Built on Cardano, Andamio delivers decentralized treasury management and contribution tracking with unmatched transparency. On-chain credentialing creates permissionless reputation systems that empower truly trustless collaboration.
