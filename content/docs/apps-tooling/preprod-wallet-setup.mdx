title: Preprod Wallet Setup
description: Set up a Cardano wallet on preprod testnet and get free test ADA
---

Before you can use Andamio on the preprod testnet, you need a Cardano wallet configured for preprod and some test ADA (tADA). This guide walks you through the setup in about 5 minutes.

## Step 1: Install a Wallet

Install one of these browser extension wallets:

- **[Lace](https://www.lace.io/)** — Modern wallet by IOG, recommended for beginners
- **[Eternl](https://eternl.io/)** — Feature-rich wallet, popular with developers
- **[Nami](https://namiwallet.io/)** — Lightweight and simple

Click the link for your preferred wallet and follow the installation instructions for your browser.

## Step 2: Create a New Wallet

After installing, open the wallet extension and create a new wallet:

1. Click **Create new wallet**
2. Write down your **recovery phrase** (12-24 words) on paper — never share this
3. Confirm your recovery phrase
4. Set a spending password

> **Keep your recovery phrase safe.** Anyone with these words can access your wallet. Store them offline, never digitally.

## Step 3: Switch to Preprod Network

By default, wallets connect to mainnet. Switch to preprod:

### Lace
1. Click the network name in the top bar (shows "Mainnet")
2. Select **Preprod**

### Eternl
1. Click the gear icon (Settings)
2. Go to **Network**
3. Select **Preprod**

### Nami
1. Click the avatar/profile icon
2. Click **Network**
3. Select **Preprod**

Your wallet now operates on the preprod testnet. Any ADA shown is test ADA with no real value.

## Step 4: Get Your Preprod Address

Copy your wallet address — you'll need it for the faucet:

1. Open your wallet extension
2. Click **Receive** or the copy icon next to your address
3. Copy the full address (starts with `addr_test1...`)

## Step 5: Get Test ADA from the Faucet

The Cardano Faucet provides free test ADA for development:

1. Go to the [Cardano Testnet Faucet](https://docs.cardano.org/cardano-testnets/tools/faucet/)
2. Select **Preprod Testnet** from the environment dropdown
3. Paste your wallet address
4. Complete the CAPTCHA
5. Click **Request funds**

The faucet sends 10,000 tADA to your wallet. Funds arrive within 1-2 minutes.

> **Faucet limits**: You can request funds once every 24 hours per address. 10,000 tADA is plenty for development and testing.

## Step 6: Verify Your Balance

Check that the funds arrived:

1. Open your wallet extension
2. Confirm you're on **Preprod** network
3. Your balance should show ~10,000 ADA

If you don't see funds after a few minutes, verify you're viewing the preprod network (not mainnet).

## Troubleshooting

### Funds not appearing
- Confirm your wallet is set to **Preprod** network
- Wait 2-3 minutes for the transaction to confirm
- Check the faucet transaction on [Cardanoscan Preprod](https://preprod.cardanoscan.io/)

### "Address invalid" error on faucet
- Make sure you copied your **preprod** address (starts with `addr_test1...`)
- Mainnet addresses start with `addr1...` and won't work on preprod

### Faucet says "already requested"
- The faucet limits requests to once per 24 hours
- If you need more tADA, create a second wallet address
