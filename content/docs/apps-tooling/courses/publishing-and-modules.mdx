---
title: Creating a Credential
description: Build a course module that defines an on-chain credential
---

A **module** defines a **credential**. The Student Learning Targets (SLTs) describe what the credential proves — what learners must demonstrate to earn it.

This guide walks you through building a module in Andamio Studio: defining SLTs, designing an assignment, and adding lessons. Once approved, the module is ready to mint on-chain as a credential.

## Prerequisites

- An Andamio access token (see [Getting Started](/docs/apps-tooling/explore-getting-started))
- A course created in Andamio Studio (see [Creating Your First Course](/docs/apps-tooling/courses/creating-your-first-course))

Give your module a title and optionally add a description. This title is what learners will see when browsing your course. Click **Create Module** to continue.

Add Student Learning Targets (SLTs) — these describe what learners must demonstrate to earn this credential. The SLTs become the credential's on-chain definition. You can save your progress at any time.

### 7. Review and Approve

Click **Review & Approve**, then **Save & Approve Module**. The SLTs are hashed — this hash becomes the credential's on-chain identifier. When minted, this is what proves a learner's achievement.
