The project treasury is an on-chain smart contract that holds ADA for task rewards. Every published task locks ADA from the treasury as a guaranteed reward. When a contributor's submission is accepted by a manager, the locked ADA is automatically distributed to the contributor's wallet.

This guide walks you through all treasury operations: funding the treasury, publishing tasks on-chain, removing tasks to reclaim funds, and understanding the deposit calculations.

### How to get here

Navigate to the Treasury Management page from the **Overview** tab by clicking the **Manage Treasury** button. You can also reach it from the **Tasks** tab when draft tasks exist.

### Page layout

The Treasury Management page has two main sections:

- **Top section**: Treasury Balance card and Add Funds component
- **Bottom section**: Two tabs, **Add to Treasury** (publish tasks) and **Remove from Treasury** (reclaim ADA)

![Full Treasury Management page showing the Treasury Balance display, the treasury address, the Add Funds input field, and the Add to Treasury tab with draft tasks listed below.](/guides/projects/treasury-management/01-treasury-full.png)

## Treasury Balance

At the top of the page, the treasury balance is displayed prominently along with the treasury address.

![Treasury Balance showing the current ADA amount, the treasury address, and the Add Funds section with an ADA amount input field.](/guides/projects/treasury-management/02-balance-card.png)

### Fields displayed

| Field | What It Shows | Why It Matters |
|-------|---------------|----------------|
| **Treasury Balance** | Current spendable ADA in the treasury (on-chain balance minus min-UTxO reserve) | Your total available budget |
| **Treasury address** | The on-chain Cardano address of this project's treasury smart contract | For independent verification on a blockchain explorer |

### Available Funds

The key number to watch is your **Available Funds**:

```
Available Funds = Treasury Balance - sum of all locked task rewards
```

Available Funds is what you can use to publish new tasks. If it is less than the total rewards you want to publish, you will need to deposit more ADA from your wallet.

## Adding Funds: Depositing ADA

The **Add Funds** section allows you to deposit ADA from your connected wallet into the project treasury.

![The Add Funds component showing the Amount (ADA) input field, a helper note explaining that funds will be available for task rewards, and the "Add to Treasury" submit button.](/guides/projects/treasury-management/03-add-funds.png)

### How to deposit

1. Enter the ADA amount you want to deposit in the **Amount (ADA)** field
2. Click **Add to Treasury**
3. Approve the transaction in your wallet
4. Wait for blockchain confirmation. The balance updates automatically

### Key details

- **Minimum deposit:** 1 ADA
- **Source:** Funds come from the connected wallet. Ensure your wallet has sufficient ADA before depositing.
- **On-chain transaction:** Deposits require a wallet signature and are recorded on the Cardano blockchain.

<Callout type="warn">
Deposited ADA goes into the project treasury smart contract. It can only be withdrawn by removing published tasks (which returns their locked ADA) or by the project owner.
</Callout>

### When to deposit

Deposit before publishing tasks whose total rewards exceed your available funds. The Transaction Summary on the Publish tab will tell you exactly how much additional ADA is needed (see below).

## Publishing Draft Tasks

The **Add to Treasury** tab is where you select draft tasks to publish on-chain. Publishing locks the reward amount for each task and makes them available to contributors.

![The Add to Treasury tab showing five draft tasks with checkboxes, task titles, on-chain hashes, and reward amounts. Three tasks are selected. Below, the Transaction Summary shows the publishing count, total rewards, treasury available, and wallet deposit required.](/guides/projects/treasury-management/04-publish-tab.png)

### Step-by-step flow

1. **Select tasks to publish**: use the checkboxes next to each draft task. A "Select All" checkbox is available in the header.
2. **Review the Transaction Summary** (see below)
3. Click **Publish Tasks** to submit the on-chain transaction
4. Approve the transaction in your wallet
5. Wait for blockchain confirmation

### Transaction Summary

The summary card at the bottom updates in real time as you select tasks:

![The Transaction Summary card showing Publishing count, Total Rewards, Treasury Available, and Wallet deposit required.](/guides/projects/treasury-management/05-transaction-summary.png)

| Summary Line | What It Shows |
|---|---|
| **Publishing** | Number of selected draft tasks |
| **Total Rewards** | Sum of ADA rewards for all selected tasks |
| **Treasury Available** | Current available ADA in the treasury |
| **Wallet deposit required** | Additional ADA needed from your wallet (0 if treasury has enough) |

### Deposit calculation

```
Wallet Deposit Required = max(0, Total Rewards - Treasury Available)
```

**Example:** You want to publish 3 tasks worth 300 ADA each (900 ADA total). Your treasury has 0 ADA available. Wallet deposit required = 900 - 0 = 900 ADA from your wallet.

### What happens on-chain

When you submit the publish transaction, several things happen atomically:

1. The required ADA is deposited from your wallet (if any)
2. The reward amount for each task is locked in the treasury
3. Each task is registered on the Cardano blockchain
4. Tasks become **Available**, visible to contributors who can commit to them

<Callout type="info">
On-chain task content is limited to 140 characters. If your task title exceeds this, it will be truncated on-chain (the full content remains in the platform database).
</Callout>

## Removing Tasks from Treasury

The **Remove from Treasury** tab allows you to remove published on-chain tasks and reclaim their locked ADA.

![The Remove from Treasury tab showing on-chain tasks with checkboxes, columns for Content (task title), Hash, Reward, and Expires date.](/guides/projects/treasury-management/06-remove-tab.png)

### Table columns

| Column | What It Shows |
|--------|---------------|
| **Content** | Task title decoded from on-chain data |
| **Hash** | On-chain transaction hash (truncated) |
| **Reward** | Locked ADA amount for this task |
| **Expires** | Task expiration date |

### How to remove tasks

1. Select tasks to remove using the checkboxes
2. Review the ADA amount that will be returned
3. Click the submit button to execute the on-chain transaction
4. Approve in your wallet and wait for confirmation
5. Locked ADA returns to your treasury balance

### When to use this

- **Cancelling tasks** that are no longer needed
- **Reclaiming ADA** from expired tasks with no accepted submissions
- **Cleaning up** the project by removing stale tasks

<Callout type="warn">
You cannot remove a task that has an active commitment (In Progress or Pending Review). Only **Available** and **Expired** tasks can be removed.
</Callout>

## Understanding the Math

All treasury calculations are derived from on-chain data:

```
Treasury Balance     = On-chain ADA balance - min-UTxO reserve
Available Funds      = Treasury Balance - sum(locked task rewards)
Deposit Required     = max(0, sum(selected task rewards) - Available Funds)
Budget Utilization   = (Rewards Distributed + Platform Fees) / Total Deposited × 100
```

### Key concepts

- **min-UTxO reserve:** A small amount of ADA (~2–3 ADA) that must remain in the UTxO for Cardano protocol requirements. This is not spendable.
- **Locked task rewards:** Every published task locks its reward amount. This ADA is committed and cannot be used for other tasks until the task is removed or its submission is accepted.
- **Platform fee (1%):** An automatic commission on distributed rewards. Deducted at the time of reward distribution, not at publishing time.
- **Budget utilization:** Visible on the [Reports tab](/docs/guides/projects/reports). Shows what percentage of your total deposits have been spent on rewards and fees.

## Workflow Quick Reference

### How to fund and publish tasks

1. Go to **Overview** → **Manage Treasury**
2. Check **Treasury Balance** and available funds
3. If you need more funds, enter an amount in **Add Funds** and click **Add to Treasury**
4. Switch to the **Add to Treasury** tab
5. Select the draft tasks to publish
6. Review the **Transaction Summary** (especially Wallet deposit required)
7. Click **Publish Tasks** and approve the wallet transaction
8. Wait for blockchain confirmation. Tasks are now **Available**

### How to check if you have enough funds

1. Note your **Treasury Balance** at the top of the page
2. Sum the rewards of the tasks you want to publish
3. If total rewards > available funds, deposit the difference using **Add Funds**

### How to reclaim funds from cancelled or expired tasks

1. Go to **Manage Treasury** → **Remove from Treasury** tab
2. Select the tasks to remove (only Available and Expired tasks can be removed)
3. Submit the on-chain transaction
4. Locked ADA returns to your treasury balance and becomes available for new tasks
