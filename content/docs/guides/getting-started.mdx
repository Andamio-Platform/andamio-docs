# Getting Started: Create Your On-Chain Identity

Before you can use Andamio as a course owner, student, project contributor, or any other role, you need to create your on-chain identity by minting an **Access Token**.

Your access token has a public alias that **cannot be changed** once minted, so choose your name carefully.

## Prerequisites

- A Cardano wallet (e.g. Lace, Eternl, Nami)
- Some test ADA if using preprod

## Walkthrough: Mint Your Access Token

### 1. Navigate to Andamio

Go to [preprod.app.andamio.io](https://preprod.app.andamio.io/) and click **Enter** to get started.

![Click Enter](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2026-02-09/949a7f7e-12dd-43b4-b4c6-65488fd2c1a7/ascreenshot_6abd8752c731441e98938bf3faffefd5_text_export.jpeg)

### 2. Connect Your Wallet

Click **Connect Wallet** and choose your wallet from the list.

![Connect Wallet](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2026-02-09/cb4c8d6c-264e-4dd6-a113-3f9ae8294001/ascreenshot_b334d8597c7042c6937d37f6033d70bc_text_export.jpeg)

![Choose Your Wallet](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2026-02-09/e6544b54-07bc-4e1b-a487-51273062e672/ascreenshot_89f9c189e7384a798f1f3f32dadeeb70_text_export.jpeg)

### 3. Choose Your Alias

Type an alias for your Access Token. This is a unique identifier and cannot be changed.

![Choose Your Alias](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2026-02-09/4650dc9b-05e2-46d2-903f-2c944327c6c7/ascreenshot_3f109ca66a454c7b88f122f30beb7ac0_text_export.jpeg)

### 4. Mint Your Access Token

Click **Mint Access Token** and sign the transaction in your wallet. Wait for the minting transaction to confirm on Cardano.

![Mint Access Token](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2026-02-09/42093a99-717f-4d7d-b56c-201c4cd32147/ascreenshot_cf851fb8d8e94b89b297c82b1bd57778_text_export.jpeg)

![Waiting for Confirmation](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2026-02-09/9e09d1f8-4dda-470a-8d3d-1743154ea043/ascreenshot_6bde4d6e53c24613b47d819d42fbd804_text_export.jpeg)

### 5. Sign In

When your access token is confirmed, click **Sign In with Your Access Token**. Connect your wallet again and sign to authenticate.
