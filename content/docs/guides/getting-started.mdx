# Getting Started: Create Your On-Chain Identity

Before you can use Andamio as a course owner, student, project contributor, or any other role, you need to create your on-chain identity by minting an **Access Token**.

Your access token has a public alias that **cannot be changed** once minted, so choose your name carefully.

## Prerequisites

- A Cardano wallet (e.g. Lace, Eternl, Nami)
- Some test ADA if using preprod

## Walkthrough: Mint Your Access Token

### 1. Navigate to Andamio

Go to [https://app.andamio.io/](https://app.andamio.io/) and click **Enter** to get started.

![Navigate to Andamio](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2026-02-08/9ca3f286-4d41-4724-9959-991f59925f45/ascreenshot_5a8d477a2c654d24b3ba8c5a525ed35a_text_export.jpeg)

![Click Enter](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2026-02-08/9ca3f286-4d41-4724-9959-991f59925f45/ascreenshot_db88d486cded423c912d7ca2d4ec207e_text_export.jpeg)

### 2. Connect Your Wallet

Click **Connect Wallet** and select your wallet provider.

![Connect Wallet](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2026-02-08/8c68b20e-ccfa-42f4-9b55-f7987d5dc9d8/ascreenshot_42f49b873bf44561b0efef6ddd32917e_text_export.jpeg)

![Select a Wallet](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2026-02-08/6dc9e280-c383-4a24-9de3-34ee4ee81806/ascreenshot_f4bf499e6b1246b88d98f3798a48cf62_text_export.jpeg)

### 3. Choose Your Access Token Alias

Type an alias for your Andamio Access Token. This alias is public and **cannot be changed**.

### 4. Mint Your Access Token

Click **Mint Access Token** and sign the transaction in your wallet. Wait for the transaction to be confirmed on-chain.

![Mint Access Token](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2026-02-08/502c9b9f-b63d-47ca-9a0a-b3193aefada5/ascreenshot_e7758c2838bf478588d595d5572bf6f9_text_export.jpeg)

### 5. Sign In

Click **Sign In with Your Access Token**, then connect your wallet again to authenticate.

![Sign In with Access Token](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2026-02-08/f50be725-542a-4dc6-bc82-533e7d7da67a/ascreenshot_6e5161c428a446b79d5bec4b70c3a45d_text_export.jpeg)

![Connect Wallet Again](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2026-02-08/dc2d1220-b89f-4474-a0f1-da08129c6240/ascreenshot_82739c11f1ed43a7aed6888c923c8c79_text_export.jpeg)

![Select Wallet](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2026-02-08/249e9e9f-30b0-4476-bb4d-4255897f5abd/ascreenshot_c39b632ed2e743dfb2021481c76033af_text_export.jpeg)

### 6. Explore Your Dashboard

You are in! From here you can view your Andamio Dashboard, browse courses, and explore the platform.

![Andamio Dashboard](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2026-02-08/98f0c24d-7101-4ae8-b62c-1cc5c23b504e/ascreenshot_c1a7c79368394df1928a38d4152f3489_text_export.jpeg)
