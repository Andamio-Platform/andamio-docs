# Getting Started: Create Your On-Chain Identity

Before you can use Andamio as a course owner, student, project contributor, or any other role, you need to create your on-chain identity by minting an **Access Token**.

Your access token is a unique identifier on the Cardano blockchain. It cannot be changed once minted, so choose your name carefully.

## Prerequisites

- A Cardano wallet (e.g. Lace, Eternl, Nami)
- Some test ADA if using preprod

## Walkthrough: Mint Your Access Token

### 1. Navigate to Andamio

Go to [https://preprod.app.andamio.io/](https://preprod.app.andamio.io/) and click **Enter**.

![Navigate to Andamio](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2026-02-08/83e7efb2-2954-4d7d-9ce3-d1b315a029b0/ascreenshot_d7b63052b88141278be13d1a3498e82e_text_export.jpeg)

![Click Enter](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2026-02-08/83e7efb2-2954-4d7d-9ce3-d1b315a029b0/ascreenshot_7297ce21a37e47018d8cbc74c66efde1_text_export.jpeg)

### 2. Connect Your Wallet

Click **Connect Wallet** and select your wallet provider.

![Connect Wallet](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2026-02-08/81cb040c-9ad1-4397-abe2-541a841ba232/ascreenshot_1f638e04dbf344eb8cd4687ff19acca4_text_export.jpeg)

![Select a Wallet](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2026-02-08/4b4f0546-f949-41e5-b3a5-7ad0a6219bb4/ascreenshot_f3c3ce27187d48e08731fcf9520e8f18_text_export.jpeg)

### 3. Choose Your Access Token Name

Enter a name for your access token. This is your unique on-chain identifier and **cannot be changed** after minting.

![Choose Access Token Name](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2026-02-08/8079a266-c7ca-4ddc-898b-7fe591484c8a/ascreenshot_af8ba3823acf46caa31ce67d61401ea5_text_export.jpeg)

### 4. Mint Your Access Token

Click **Mint Access Token** and sign the transaction in your wallet.

![Mint Access Token](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2026-02-08/d194216b-5edc-4b53-b415-dcc63f379ab4/ascreenshot_f22cf750c94343ff8d2bb926a11ca6ba_text_export.jpeg)

### 5. Sign In

Once your token is minted, click **Sign In with Your Access Token**, then connect your wallet again to authenticate.

![Sign In with Access Token](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2026-02-08/6fc657f3-6f8f-4fce-b67e-63d4d6ae0624/ascreenshot_7fce306eea6a4fb6aa7e03a2e70f0d9a_text_export.jpeg)

![Connect Wallet Again](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2026-02-08/387c1e7a-229f-445e-90b7-c9eae474ae93/ascreenshot_5ec680661f82429883ebba4ee9fcad25_text_export.jpeg)

![Select Wallet](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2026-02-08/dc45bf9b-d5f5-425d-8c0a-e525b7bfb751/ascreenshot_cd3e24ab7bf44cb39984c5c36b90d367_text_export.jpeg)

### 6. Explore Andamio

You're in! From here you can browse courses, check your learning dashboard, or explore the platform.
