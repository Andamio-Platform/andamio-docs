# Creating Your First Course

This guide walks you through creating a course in Andamio Studio and minting it on-chain. Once your course exists, you can add modules with credentials that students can earn.

Minting a course costs approximately **150 ADA**.

## Prerequisites

- An Andamio access token (see [Getting Started](/docs/guides/getting-started))
- **Course Owner permissions** granted by the instance owner

## Walkthrough: Create a Course

### 1. Navigate to Course Studio

From your dashboard, click **Course Studio** in the navigation.

![Your Andamio Dashboard](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2026-02-09/d29faa09-9044-4f07-aac4-57a3afab275e/ascreenshot_9e2b2b18863f42ed919e57c72f0fb20f_text_export.jpeg)

![Click Course Studio in the sidebar](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2026-02-09/d29faa09-9044-4f07-aac4-57a3afab275e/ascreenshot_40acf2c7c7c34041ab188f5fc0b71d00_text_export.jpeg)

### 2. Create a Course

Click **Create a Course** and choose a public title for your course. You can change this later.

![Click Create a Course](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2026-02-09/8006ea3e-0a49-45ad-b7a3-4a04180e5851/ascreenshot_9bda3390bd6e44b186f85bcf52941643_text_export.jpeg)

![Enter a title for your course](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2026-02-09/9ea333f2-fdea-46cf-8942-282781240a93/ascreenshot_57dc16b09d24483e90c9d9cf52ad0aac_text_export.jpeg)

### 3. Mint the Course NFT

Click **Mint Course NFT** and sign the transaction in your wallet. Wait for the transaction to confirm on the blockchain.

![Click Mint Course NFT](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2026-02-09/22994b53-0777-46ab-b1bb-47f37568df3c/ascreenshot_1520cbdbcea2414299a8d00593fb096d_text_export.jpeg)

![Waiting for transaction confirmation](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2026-02-09/d87f837d-c78d-4ee2-b74b-9b8fff149c53/ascreenshot_c3af1be091d34ecb87f80d3084bf83a9_text_export.jpeg)

### 4. Open Your Course

Select your new course in the Studio navigation menu. You're now ready to create your first on-chain credential.

![Select your course from the navigation](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2026-02-09/a2a53beb-c8e2-41ab-92ed-a064b5e9dcee/ascreenshot_326731f8ffe74204be9a3787bc4922ae_text_export.jpeg)

![Your course overview — ready to add modules](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2026-02-09/fe8310ed-5b68-4bc5-81ed-edcd6f41fe4b/ascreenshot_4a9fe14ad6b14ce6a4accad24ec30439_text_export.jpeg)
