# Creating Your First Course

<Callout type="info">
This guide is under active development.
</Callout>

## What You'll Learn

- How to set up your course structure
- Creating a course on-chain
- Adding your first modules
- Opening enrollment for students

## Prerequisites

Before creating a course, you'll need:

1. An **Andamio Access Token** ([mint one here](/docs/protocol/v2/transactions/global/general/access-token/mint))
2. **Course Owner permissions** granted by the instance owner
3. A connected Cardano wallet

## Walkthrough: Create a Course and Module

### 1. Navigate to Andamio Studio

Go to [https://preprod.app.andamio.io/](https://preprod.app.andamio.io/) and click **Enter**.

![Navigate to Andamio](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2026-02-08/946b37a4-367c-48f7-90e8-1839d9c2fcc4/ascreenshot_476f690109e1430a8b74389597594341_text_export.jpeg)

![Click Enter](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2026-02-08/946b37a4-367c-48f7-90e8-1839d9c2fcc4/ascreenshot_d04a0cf3aa3a4ac194d84180d8f98f56_text_export.jpeg)

### 2. Connect Your Wallet

Click **Connect Wallet** and select your wallet provider.

![Connect Wallet](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2026-02-08/7f5c12fe-c0e0-407d-abbe-bdbfcf69c213/ascreenshot_a903e756f62f4ab69ec776e6498b12d9_text_export.jpeg)

![Select Wallet](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2026-02-08/25ae142b-140f-46be-8a66-aa35ac5fc82d/ascreenshot_70f4412890644fda806a208689e66a44_text_export.jpeg)

### 3. Open Course Studio

Click **Course Studio** from the navigation.

![Course Studio](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2026-02-08/64d09cc9-b839-4eeb-84ab-bbff4042dd4e/ascreenshot_e1d303b04a6f4933aef3ab647ca2f7b4_text_export.jpeg)

### 4. Create the Course

Click **Create a Course**, enter a title (e.g. "My New Course"), then click **Mint Course NFT**.

![Create a Course](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2026-02-08/5ded94f3-2961-4135-bfe2-88b478b2779c/ascreenshot_d4a2a8ac395b4d049428038a4c028746_text_export.jpeg)

![Enter Course Title](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2026-02-08/977c264f-796c-481d-88a8-f9d40557dc11/ascreenshot_67012e45819b40ba91305f1ecaa43d34_text_export.jpeg)

![Mint Course NFT](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2026-02-08/59c33623-3c68-4c5d-91b2-41efc33aa0d4/ascreenshot_a0d1d54826764690b63d07f7a42ed73c_text_export.jpeg)

### 5. Start Building Modules

Click your new course, then click **Start Building**.

![Select Course](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2026-02-08/f9638dfd-0c5f-4f63-922d-7aa3856d0f77/ascreenshot_c580af2adb3943f1b9c264c458c86db5_text_export.jpeg)

![Start Building](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2026-02-08/ef2a3b67-27a2-4690-a1e9-67de16d06f21/ascreenshot_b7ba8794d06148c194c6edeac9e41056_text_export.jpeg)

### 6. Create a Module

Enter a **Module Title** (e.g. "My First Module") and a **Description**, then click **Create Module**.

![Module Title](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2026-02-08/ec07ace6-2005-4a2d-bab4-e4524330f424/ascreenshot_8d5f78bc99c346f2a1fc03f6bf599f42_text_export.jpeg)

![Module Description](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2026-02-08/ab95c428-bd07-4e19-b400-09f17e0fb12b/ascreenshot_a71a1aa616c9491589b605688ef57fc5_text_export.jpeg)

![Create Module](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2026-02-08/c76f48a2-2fd2-4281-9143-a3b6d0e2b368/ascreenshot_55a338a83e214d069939d95507b98e5a_text_export.jpeg)

### 7. Add Student Learning Targets (SLTs)

Enter SLTs that describe what students will be able to do after completing the module. Click **Add** after each one.

![Add SLT](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2026-02-08/a5bf146c-70fc-4d06-8cca-896eff455df1/ascreenshot_1e5183214b2c465ab65e0d434fc891f5_text_export.jpeg)

![Add Another SLT](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2026-02-08/8f9e31b2-7896-46be-9cb9-5428f60a4063/ascreenshot_5f2f2283177c4996b5bf3d38db7414d2_text_export.jpeg)

### 8. Design the Assignment

Click **Design the Assignment**. Use the editor to describe what students need to do to prove they've met the SLTs.

![Design the Assignment](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2026-02-08/3bd6caba-46d8-4e2a-969a-aba908126030/ascreenshot_2c3551f636c84979b5a933e9e81c0c0a_text_export.jpeg)

![Assignment Editor](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2026-02-08/b423846a-4840-49b3-851d-da8a2d47db59/ascreenshot_4dca2f57c93b4e58b1b3f9541f9876e1_text_export.jpeg)

![Write Assignment Content](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2026-02-08/9ec5741b-4c58-4ed9-aca7-b221746ae442/ascreenshot_33b58d7a811a444b8719141561479f0e_text_export.jpeg)

Click **Save** when done.

![Save Assignment](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2026-02-08/12880304-813c-4c9b-b279-d782f03bd21c/ascreenshot_ed3aa61c0d944d8d89b8eacac11c7ee5_text_export.jpeg)

### 9. Write the Introduction

Click **Introduction** and add welcome content for your module. Click **Save** when done.

![Introduction Tab](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2026-02-08/6ec37381-ba07-4f92-8126-bcad5d5aa717/ascreenshot_0801232392a9452ab06f60eaffa5d297_text_export.jpeg)

![Write Introduction](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2026-02-08/88c6b390-43dd-4c2c-ba96-0b72b6b8d608/ascreenshot_80fa0b3596bd48bfa127cc5e007c6864_text_export.jpeg)

![Save Introduction](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2026-02-08/b1a83d13-1187-49d9-bbee-19d89d7ecb6c/ascreenshot_b758d8a855b040a7bbe67cc9773f77c6_text_export.jpeg)

### 10. Approve and Mint the Module

Click **Save & Approve Module** to finalize the module content.

![Save & Approve Module](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2026-02-08/6202b193-4060-4202-985f-e1f84682eb51/ascreenshot_3493d41d3f1840ac988ee9fa5078da56_text_export.jpeg)

Click **Ready to Mint →** to proceed to minting.

![Ready to Mint](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2026-02-08/2c40be4d-91e9-404f-bb37-e7245489f1d8/ascreenshot_ee31987883a74fffa33f389136bffd34_text_export.jpeg)

Review the module details and SLT token names, then click **Mint 1 Module** to publish the module on-chain.

![Review Module](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2026-02-08/967a4229-dba2-4148-acc7-3685443c1bb0/ascreenshot_0699216bd1c04720860a00a5118b8ecc_text_export.jpeg)

![Mint Module](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2026-02-08/113f5968-60cd-476e-ba6e-66ad6ef8356f/ascreenshot_eee8459ee5444b8bb5de6b6753956ad9_text_export.jpeg)
