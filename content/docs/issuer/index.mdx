Andamio Issuer adds a verifiable credential to the programs your organization already runs. You define what a credential certifies, your learners submit evidence, your teachers accept it, and the learner claims a credential that anyone can verify on a public ledger.

Each credential lives on a public blockchain, so it is permanent, belongs to the person who earned it, and anyone can verify it without asking you. Andamio Issuer is a lightweight API that gives you all of that without touching crypto. Andamio builds and pays for every transaction, and people sign with a social login or a browser wallet, so nobody buys or holds ADA.

## Who it's for

Organizations that issue credentials and need them to mean something to whoever relies on them: an employer, a partner, or their own team. If you run a certification program, a training track, or a partner program today, Issuer turns the badges you hand out into credentials your systems, and others, can act on.

## Why it's different from a badge

A typical digital badge is a picture in a vendor's database. It can be changed, switched off, or lost, and most badges carry no data a system can read. An Andamio credential is the opposite:

## What it takes to start

- A plan, sized to your cohort.
- A developer to build the screens your people use. Issuer has no learner or reviewer app.
- The final, approved statements for each credential.
- Your site's address registered for social login, which Andamio allowlists before your key is issued.

[Before you start](/docs/issuer/before-you-start) lists every decision and setup step.
