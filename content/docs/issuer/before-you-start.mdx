---
title: Before you start
description: The decisions and setup to have ready before you issue a credential
---

Most of what you need ready are decisions, not setup. The credential text takes longest, because it can never change once it is on chain.

## A plan and someone to order it

Order a plan in the [Issuer app](https://issuer.andamio.io). Know your cohort size first. A plan has three allocations:

| Allocation | What uses one |
|---|---|
| **Users** | Each account you create for a person with `POST /accounts/create` |
| **Credentials** | Each learner whose evidence a teacher accepts |
| **Courses** | Each course you create with `POST /courses/create` |

A Credentials unit is drawn when the accept transaction is submitted, and it is refunded if that transaction does not land. A batch that accepts ten learners needs ten units, or the whole batch is refused with `402 QUOTA_EXHAUSTED`. Read what is left with `GET /quota`.

## A developer

Andamio Issuer is an API. It has no learner or reviewer app, so a developer builds the screens your people use: where a learner submits evidence, where a teacher accepts it, and where a learner claims the credential.

## Which programs become courses

Every credential belongs to a course. Decide which of your programs become courses, and which credentials each course issues.

## The final statements for each credential

A credential is an ordered list of statements, and the credential is the hash of that list. A changed word or a new order makes a different credential, and nothing can be edited on mainnet. Get each list written, approved and put in order before you define it. [How it works](/docs/issuer/how-it-works) has the rules for a statement.

## What learners submit as evidence, and where you keep it

Only a short text of up to 140 characters goes on chain with each submission. A hash of the evidence is recommended, and a public URL is fine where the evidence is already public. That text is public and permanent. Never put personal data in it.

The evidence itself stays with you. Decide where you store it, how long you keep it and how you delete it when someone asks.

## A course owner and teachers

Name a course owner from your own staff, and the teachers who will accept evidence. Each one needs an alias held in a wallet, through a social login or a browser wallet. Aliases are public and permanent, so choose them with that in mind.

## Your site's address for social login

Social login lets people sign without installing a wallet. It works only on a site address Andamio has allowlisted. Register the address (for example `app.yourcompany.com`) in the [Issuer app](https://issuer.andamio.io). An Andamio operator still allowlists it by hand, and your key is issued after that, so register it early.

## A privacy notice

Show a privacy notice before anyone connects a wallet. It names who controls the data.
