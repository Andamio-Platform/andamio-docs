---
title: Support
description: Where an Andamio Issuer customer asks a question
---

Email [support@andamio.io](mailto:support@andamio.io). Andamio replies within one business day.

To get an answer faster, include:

- The email you signed up to the Issuer app with
- The network, preprod or mainnet
- The route you called and when
- The error `code` and `message` from the response, if there was one

Never send your API key.

The [Issuer API reference](https://api.andamio.io/issuer/v1/docs/index.html) has the full request and response schema for every route.
